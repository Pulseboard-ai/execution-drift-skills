# Outreach templates

Write in the user's voice. Short, one ask each, a date on every ask. Adjust for anything in `stakeholders.md`.

## SME ask (to fill in options)
> <Name> — quick one on <blocker>. I'm putting options in front of <leader> by <date> and need your read on two things: (1) <specific question>, (2) <specific question>. Rough numbers are fine; I'll label them as estimates. Can you get me that by <date/time>?

## Pre-wire 1:1 (affected stakeholder, before the room)
> <Name> — heads up before <meeting/date>. <Blocker, one sentence>. I'm proposing three options: <A>, <B>, <C>. I'm leaning <X> because <reason>. <Your team> is affected by <specific impact>. Anything I'm missing, or a reason to prefer another one? Want to make sure you're not seeing this for the first time in the room.

## Decision request (cover note for the memo)
> <Leader> — one decision needed by <date> on <blocker>. Memo attached, one page. Recommendation is <X>; <names> have seen it and <agree / have one concern noted>. If I don't hear back by <date> we'll proceed with <X>.

## Notify dependents (after the decision)
> <Team/channel> — decision on <blocker>: we're going with <X>. What changes for you: <one line>. Effective <date>. Questions to <owner>. <link to memo>

## Team-level resolution (no escalation)
> <Team> — <blocker> resolved at team level: <option>, owner <name>, done by <date>. Not escalating because <reason>. Logged.
