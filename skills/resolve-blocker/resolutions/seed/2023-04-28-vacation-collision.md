Blocker: Three key engineers on planned vacation across the same two weeks that overlapped the release window.
Gap: Release date set without checking the team's leave calendar.
Options:
  - A: Move the release two weeks
  - B: Ask people to shift vacation — trust cost, and two had bookings
  - C: Pull the release-critical work forward, name a coverage owner per area, keep the date
Chosen: C
Why: Only three tasks were truly on the critical path; they fit before the leave started.
Escalated: no
Time to resolve: 1 day to re-sequence
Outcome: Released on date. Leave calendar is now an input to every release-date decision.
