Blocker: Engineer owning the hardest module resigned mid-feature with two weeks' notice.
Gap: Single-owner module; no design doc.
Options:
  - A: Backfill and ramp — 8+ weeks
  - B: Use the notice period for structured handoff: design doc, pairing with a named successor, recorded walkthrough
  - C: Reassign the module to a contractor
Chosen: B
Why: Two weeks of pairing preserved most of the context; the successor was already adjacent.
Escalated: no — EM decision
Time to resolve: 2 weeks handoff, ~2 weeks slip on the module
Outcome: Successor delivered with one extra sprint. Handoff checklist became standard for any departure.
