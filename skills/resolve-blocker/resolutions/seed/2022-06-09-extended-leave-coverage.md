Blocker: A lead going on planned multi-month leave owned two in-flight initiatives.
Gap: Leave was known; ownership transfer wasn't planned until four weeks out.
Options:
  - A: Acting lead covers both initiatives on top of their own work
  - B: Split: one initiative to an acting lead, the other paused with a stated restart date
  - C: Hire a contractor lead for the duration
Chosen: B
Why: Pausing the lower-priority initiative openly beat two initiatives limping.
Escalated: yes — Director, because a pause is a roadmap change
Time to resolve: 1 week to decide and transfer
Outcome: The active initiative delivered on plan; the paused one restarted on the stated date. Ownership transfer now starts 8 weeks before any planned leave.
