Blocker: Tech lead spending ~60% of time onboarding two hires; feature velocity halved for the quarter's committed item.
Gap: Plan assumed full lead capacity; onboarding load never in the capacity model.
Options:
  - A: Hold date, accept quality risk
  - B: Move date 3 weeks, keep scope
  - C: Assign a senior IC as onboarding buddy for 4 weeks, lead returns to feature, date moves 1 week
Chosen: C
Why: Smallest slip; buddy role also built bench depth. One-way door on the date (already communicated to a customer) so a 1-week slip needed VP sign-off.
Escalated: yes — VP Eng, because the date had been shared externally
Time to resolve: 3 days to decision, 5 weeks to done
Outcome: Delivered 1 week late as forecast. Capacity model now carries an onboarding line per hire.
