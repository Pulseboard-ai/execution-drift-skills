Blocker: Quarter commitment planned at 6 engineers; only 4 available after two were pulled to an incident-driven priority.
Gap: Capacity plan had no reserve; reassignment happened without re-planning the commitment.
Options:
  - A: Hold scope and date, absorb with overtime — burnout, quality risk
  - B: Descope two lower-value items, hold date on the core
  - C: Borrow two engineers from a sister team for 4 weeks with a defined return date
Chosen: B
Why: Borrowing had a ramp cost that ate half the benefit; descoped items had no external commitment.
Escalated: yes — Director, to sign off the descope publicly
Time to resolve: 2 days to decision
Outcome: Core delivered on date; descoped items shipped the following quarter. Capacity plans now carry a 15% reserve line.
