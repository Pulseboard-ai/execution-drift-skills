Blocker: Tech lead spending most of their time ramping two new hires; velocity on the quarter's committed item halved.
Gap: Capacity model assumed full lead availability; onboarding load wasn't costed.
Options:
  - A: Hold the date, accept quality risk
  - B: Move the date 3 weeks
  - C: Assign a senior IC as onboarding buddy for 4 weeks; lead returns to the feature; date moves 1 week
Chosen: C
Why: Smallest slip; buddy role also built bench depth. Date had been shared with a customer, so even a 1-week move needed sign-off.
Escalated: yes — VP Eng, because of the external date
Time to resolve: 3 days to decision
Outcome: Delivered 1 week late as forecast. Capacity model now includes an onboarding line per hire.
